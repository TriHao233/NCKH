QUESTION TYPE: sap_xep (Ordering / Sequencing)

RULES:
1. Extract a real process, algorithm flow, or logical sequence directly from the context. Do NOT invent steps that are not in the text.
2. Present the steps SHUFFLED and numbered as "1", "2", "3", "4", etc.
3. There must be at least 4 steps.
4. The `options` field in JSON must list the shuffled steps. Example:
   "options": {"1": "Step X", "2": "Step Y", "3": "Step Z", "4": "Step W"}
5. The `correct_answer` field MUST provide the correct chronological sequence of the keys, separated by commas. Example: "3, 1, 4, 2".
6. Use this type only when the context contains a genuine order, workflow, algorithm, lifecycle, priority sequence, or dependency chain.
7. Each step must be concise, atomic, and uniquely placeable. Do not split one action into duplicate micro-steps just to reach 4 items.
8. The stem should ask students to arrange the process itself, not to arrange sentences "from the document".
