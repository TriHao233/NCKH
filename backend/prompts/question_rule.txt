FORBIDDEN QUESTION RULES:

1. Do not create questions that ask students to remember the document's location or structure, including chapter, section, part, page, table of contents, content order, or where an idea appears.

2. Do not create source-referencing questions. Forbidden wording includes "Theo tài liệu", "Theo chương", "Theo giáo trình", "Theo tác giả", "Dựa vào nội dung trên", "Trong tài liệu", and any equivalent phrasing.

3. Do not ask about document metadata or visual/formal features, including file name, page count, title as metadata, font, color, layout position, or presentation style.

4. Do not ask students to memorize verbatim wording or trivial details that do not represent meaningful subject knowledge.

5. Do not create questions that depend on external conversational context such as "the example above", "the previous paragraph", "the figure above", "as mentioned", or similar references. Each question must be semantically self-contained.

6. Do not create vague questions, under-specified questions, or questions without a determinate correct answer.

7. For single-choice questions, do not create more than one correct answer, and do not create options that are duplicated or nearly equivalent in meaning.

8. Do not create distractors that are absurd, unrelated, or too easy to eliminate.

9. Do not include any knowledge in the question, options, correct answer, or explanation that is unsupported by the source context. This includes hallucinated facts, outside technologies, outside programming languages, or examples not grounded in the context.

GENERAL RULE: Questions must assess real knowledge, stand independently from the source document, be clear, evidence-based, and valuable for assessment. If any rule is violated, reject the item and generate a replacement.
