BLOOM LEVEL: VAN_DUNG (Applying)

DEMAND: The ability to apply learned information, rules, or formulas to new situations, novel conditions, and practical problem-solving.
KEY VERBS TO TARGET: Apply, calculate, manipulate, operate, solve, use, practice, demonstrate.

RULE: 
- Present a concrete, practical scenario or a new condition based on the context. 
- The student must select the correct application of a learned concept, formula, or theory to solve the given problem.
- The scenario may change surface details, but the rule, method, syntax, constraint, or procedure being applied must come from the context.
- The correct answer should require choosing what to do next, which rule to use, or which result follows from applying the learned knowledge.
