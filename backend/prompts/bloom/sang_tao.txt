BLOOM LEVEL: SANG_TAO (Creating)

DEMAND: The ability to put elements together to form a novel, coherent whole or make an original product/solution.
KEY VERBS TO TARGET: Design, propose, construct, combine, plan, invent, develop, formulate.

RULE: 
- The question should challenge the student to synthesize ideas into a new structure, plan, or alternative solution.
- The student must go beyond the basic text, applying principles to construct a solution that meets specific constraints.
- Require the learner to assemble multiple ideas, steps, rules, or components from the context into a coherent plan, design, algorithm, outline, or improvement.
- The expected answer must still be bounded by the context; do not ask for open-ended invention that cannot be judged from the provided evidence.
