BLOOM LEVEL: PHAN_TICH (Analyzing)

DEMAND: The ability to break information into its component parts and determine how the parts relate to one another and to an overall structure.
KEY VERBS TO TARGET: Analyze, break down, compare, contrast, deduce, differentiate, investigate, examine.

RULE: 
- The question must require identifying relationships, underlying structures, or root causes.
- The student must demonstrate understanding of how different parts of the knowledge interact with each other.
- Ask about why one component affects another, how steps depend on each other, how concepts differ under specific conditions, or what cause leads to a stated effect.
- Avoid questions that can be answered by naming a single definition; the learner must separate parts, compare alternatives, or infer a relationship grounded in the context.
