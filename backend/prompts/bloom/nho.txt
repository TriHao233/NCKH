BLOOM LEVEL: NHO (Remembering)

DEMAND: The ability to recall information and facts without necessarily understanding their underlying meaning.
KEY VERBS TO TARGET: Define, describe, list, name, identify, present, state, relate.

RULE: 
- Ask directly about terms, facts, or concepts. 
- The question must test if the student can recall specific information directly from the learned material.
- Focus on precise identifiers, definitions, properties, commands, syntax, roles, or steps that appear in the context.
- Avoid vague memory checks; each answer must be a specific item that can be verified from the context.
- Do not ask for deep explanations or complex problem-solving.
