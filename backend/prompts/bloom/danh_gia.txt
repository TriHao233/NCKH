BLOOM LEVEL: DANH_GIA (Evaluating)

DEMAND: The ability to make judgments and decisions about the value of information, ideas, or methods based on specific criteria and standards.
KEY VERBS TO TARGET: Evaluate, conclude, defend, judge, justify, decide, recommend, criticize.

RULE: 
- Provide a scenario with multiple approaches or a statement that requires critical thinking.
- The student must critique, justify, or evaluate a choice or position using evidence-based reasoning derived from the context.
- The question must include or imply clear evaluation criteria, such as correctness, efficiency, safety, maintainability, suitability, or trade-offs.
- Do not ask for simple recall, definition, listing, or one-step recognition. The learner must compare alternatives or judge a claim.
- The correct answer and explanation must be grounded in the provided context. If the context does not provide enough evidence for a high-quality evaluating question, generate the closest lower-risk question that still asks for justified judgment from available evidence.
- For multiple-choice questions, make distractors plausible but clearly weaker when judged against the criteria in the context.
