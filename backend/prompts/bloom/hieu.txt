BLOOM LEVEL: HIEU (Understanding)

DEMAND: The ability to grasp the meaning of information and explain the learned concepts.
KEY VERBS TO TARGET: Explain, distinguish, interpret, summarize, illustrate, paraphrase, discuss, classify.

RULE: 
- Test the student's ability to interpret, paraphrase, or summarize ideas. 
- The question should require the student to explain "WHY" or "HOW" a concept works, rather than just recognizing its name.
- Ask students to connect a concept with its purpose, behavior, condition, consequence, or category using the meaning stated in the context.
- Distractors should reflect common misunderstandings of the concept, not unrelated facts or keyword swaps.
